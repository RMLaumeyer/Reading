## Recoveries

Civil War II: Choosing Sides gained original-run corroboration from [Marvel’s creator listing of issue #6](https://www.marvel.com/comics/creators/1113/sana_takeda). Its old rewrite incorrectly made a major loss the cause of the division. The source describes disagreement over advance knowledge and prevention as the dividing question; the loss then makes heroes reconsider their choices. The revision repairs that causal relationship and restores the named participants and global anthology scope.

Luke Cage: Caged! gained [original 2017 issue corroboration](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/64925). A revision replaces a dangling pronoun, repairs damaged contractions and retains the prison, mine and hospital dangers without inventing a controller, illness, escape or family outcome.

Sabretooth: The Dead Don’t Talk gained [original 2024 issue corroboration](https://www.marvel.com/comics/issue/121362/sabretooth_the_dead_dont_talk_2024_1). The revision restores Victor Creed as the subject and keeps his historical gangster story separate from possible effects on Wolverine in the present. It removes the old rewrite’s unsupported treatment of named individuals as gangs and does not identify Mad Dog Murphy with any other character.

Captain America and Black Widow remains held: publisher collection evidence has not established its original 2012 namespace. Spider-Gwen: Predators retains its 2015 versus explicit 2015B identity concern and weak plot-context hold. Neither was cleared just because a collecting range looked plausible.
