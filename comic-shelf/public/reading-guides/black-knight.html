Where to begin.

This guide follows Dane Whitman, the Avenger, rather than Sir Percy or Nathan Garrett. His key stories often live in team books. Start with Curse of the Ebony Blade for a compact modern introduction, or Captain Britain and MI13 for a team run.

How to read this guide

Chronological means original story publication order, not the catalog edition or reprint year. Entries follow their first publication year; overlapping runs may cover the same period. This is a curated selection of starting points and key stories, not a complete issue-by-issue chronology. Alternate settings and continuity are labeled. A team-book recommendation on a member’s page explains that member’s role; it is not a solo series.

Library matches use your supplied catalog snapshot, imported September 8, 2026. “Hoopla listed” is not a live borrowing check. “No confirmed match” is a gap in this cross-reference, not proof that the library lacks the story. Read collection notes: some books are alternatives, others are consecutive volumes or cover only part of a larger saga. Recommendations reflect the linked readers and critics rather than a measured consensus. Story titles may reveal major milestones.
